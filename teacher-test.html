Part B · Short answers (8 marks)

  • 9. Fourteen; nineteen. One mark each.
  • 10. Her father is a teacher; her mother works at a bank. One mark each.
  • 11. Any two accurate commands from Unit 3, such as “Keep your body straight,” “Keep your head still,” or “Keep your arms forward.” One mark each.
  • 12. The grasshopper cannot find food / becomes weak or starves because he did not store food during summer. One mark for what happens; one for why.

Part C · Textbook activities (7 marks)

  • 13. B → E → D → A → C. One mark for each step in the right position.
  • 14. “Friday and Saturday are their holidays.” One mark for the capital letters; one for the final full stop.

Part D · Teaching preparation (7 marks)

  • 15. 1 mark each for a suitable greeting, name/class information, a relevant question and response, and a polite closing. Accept natural variations.
  • 16. 1.5 marks per suitable question-and-accurate-answer pair based on the water-pollution lesson. Examples: “What did Shihab and Kaniz see in the river? — Rubbish and plastic.” / “Why should we not throw rubbish into the river? — It pollutes the water and can harm people and fish.”
